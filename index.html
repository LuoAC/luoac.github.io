Aocheng Luo

Ph.D. Student @ Peking University/Navigation Algorithm Intern @ Light Origins

I am Aocheng Luo, a Ph.D. student at the School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics, Peking University, advised by Prof. Junzhi Yu (IEEE Fellow). I am currently a Navigation Algorithm Intern at Light Origins, where I work on supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) post-training for LightNav-0, a generalist embodied navigation model.

My research focuses on embodied navigation: how multimodal large models and reinforcement learning can help robots understand instructions, reason about space and move robustly in open, unstructured environments. Before that, I worked on locomotion and local navigation for legged robots, combining model-based control and RL with depth-based perception.

I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering, with a minor in Artificial Intelligence, from Beijing Institute of Technology, ranking 1st of 86 students.

Featured Research

arXiv 2026 Light Origins Open-source

LightNav-0: Eliciting VLM Spatial Intelligence for Generalist Embodied Navigation

Shaoan Wang, Aocheng Luo, Fei Huang, Jingyi Xu, et al. (Light Origins Team)

A compact generalist navigation model built on a vision-language model. Dual-channel pointing and a residual vector-quantized action tokenizer let a single model handle instruction following, open-vocabulary object navigation and visual tracking. It reaches state-of-the-art monocular success rates on all 10 public navigation simulation settings and transfers zero-shot to humanoid, quadruped, wheeled and aerial robots.

arXiv 2026 VLA Reasoning & Memory

VLingNav: Embodied Navigation with Adaptive Reasoning and Visual-Assisted Linguistic Memory

Shaoan Wang, Yuanfei Luo, Xingyu Chen, Aocheng Luo, Dongyue Li, Chang Liu, Sheng Chen, Yangang Zhang, Junzhi Yu

A vision-language-action (VLA) model for embodied navigation. An adaptive chain-of-thought (AdaCoT) mechanism triggers explicit reasoning only when needed, and a visual-assisted linguistic memory (VLingMem) lets the agent recall what it has seen over long horizons. Trained on Nav-AdaCoT-2.9M and refined with online expert-guided RL, it achieves state-of-the-art results on object navigation, image navigation and visual tracking benchmarks and transfers zero-shot to real robots.

IEEE T-ASE 2026 First Author Quadruped RL

Perceptive Locomotion and Navigation for Quadruped Robots via Depth-Based Representation

Aocheng Luo, Dongyue Li, Shaoan Wang, Shihan Kong, Kaiwei Zhu, Junzhi Yu

A hierarchical, map-free RL framework: a navigation policy turns raw depth images into velocity commands, and a locomotion policy combines proprioception with depth features distilled from privileged terrain scans into a lightweight MobileViT-GRU encoder. Contrastive forward prediction and inverse dynamics improve representation learning, and a Warp-based GPU-parallel depth renderer for Isaac Gym renders depth nearly 43× faster than the built-in camera. The policy transfers zero-shot to a real Lite3 quadruped for obstacle avoidance, stair climbing and map-free navigation.
